ALVIE in your browser

ALVIE learns models of processors and services and compares them to find timing side channels. Here it runs entirely in your browser: nothing to install, and your files and models stay on your computer.

    Empty session

    VS Code with the specifications and models of the experiments, ALVIE terminals, and a preview of models. Your files are kept between visits.

The first time you open a tutorial, VS Code asks whether you trust the authors of the notebook: the tutorials come with this site, so choose Open Notebook (tick Don't ask me again to not be asked for the others). Their cells run only commands of ALVIE.

ALVIE and the simulators of Sancus are WebAssembly; they need a recent browser with WebAssembly GC (Chromium is the fastest). In a session, ALVIE: Reset the Files to Those of a First Visit (in the Command Palette, Ctrl+Shift+P) brings back the original files.
